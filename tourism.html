Tourism in Temporis

Visitors are welcome to the city, its fairs, its markets, and its public landmarks.

Welcome to Temporis

Temporis welcomes travelers, merchants, scholars, and artisans from many lands. The city is known for its public works, civic order, and its willingness to receive guests who respect local custom and civic law.

Falxians, the citizens of Temporis, are especially welcoming to visitors. They value outside trade, shared celebration, and the presence of new people within the city and its public life.

Visitors are encouraged to consult public notices before arriving, particularly when access to a landmark or route is restricted for repairs or safety.

The World Trade Fair

The World Trade Fair is Temporis's principal civic festival and the city's main seasonal gathering. It welcomes visitors from across the world for commerce, public celebration, and competition.

The fair is held over two days and features a different competition each year, while maintaining the broader public festival and market atmosphere for which the event is known.

History of The World Trade Fair

The first World Trade Fair was marked by a clothing competition with a Goblin theme on the first day and a Paladin theme on the second. The event established the fair as a public celebration of craft, spectacle, and civic exchange.

Travelers, merchants, and artisans are invited to participate in the fair and to experience the city's public hospitality.

The Cathedral

The Cathedral is one of Temporis's defining landmarks. It is currently undergoing reconstruction, and access may be restricted at times while work continues.

Visitors are advised to consult public notices before planning a visit to the Cathedral grounds.

What Visitors Should Know

  • Respect civic rules and public notices.
  • Check for access restrictions before entering active work sites or restricted civic buildings.
  • Expect a city that values order, record keeping, and public safety.
  • Public events are encouraged and welcomed when conducted under city customs and safety requirements.

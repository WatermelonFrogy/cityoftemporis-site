The Cathedral

A landmark of Temporis, currently under reconstruction.

← Return to the civic portal

A Notable Feature of the City

The Cathedral is among the most notable features of Temporis and holds a central place in the city's fellowship with the New Church of Jonas Falx. It has long stood as a landmark for residents and visitors alike.

Reconstruction

Rebuilding of the Cathedral is currently underway, guided by findings from ongoing archaeological surveys of the original structure. The work is overseen by the Department of Municipal Works, with findings from the surveys informing how the structure is restored.

Progress Updates

Updates on the reconstruction are issued as public notices by the Department of Municipal Works as work continues.

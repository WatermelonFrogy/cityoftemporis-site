About the City

The Independent City of Temporis governs itself under charter while remaining in fellowship with the New Church of Jonas Falx. Most residents are Seraphs. The city calls on the Forlorn Hope, its knightly order, for defense in matters both routine and dire.

Temporis has declared its independence from the Conclave of Hagia, a political union of several other settlements on the continent. The city seeks neither to rule its neighbors nor to be ruled by them, and remains open to trade, diplomacy, and alliances built on mutual respect between equals.

The Forlorn Hope

The knightly order and standing guard of Temporis.

“While one stands, hope remains.”Creed of the Forlorn Hope

What We Are

The Forlorn Hope are the knights of Temporis. Not lords, not a bloodline, and not a reward for birth. We are a duty that anyone can be called to and no one is owed.

Our name comes from the knights who stood upon the surface of a dying world while humanity sheltered beneath it. They held the line because someone had to.

Our Three Duties

Protect

Protect the weak and stand between danger and those who would suffer from it.

Preserve

Preserve knowledge, records, techniques, names, and lives from needless loss.

Endure

Remain steady when fear, danger, or uncertainty would make standing difficult.

Service in the Hope

The Hope serves across the walls and roads of Temporis. Squires and Knights may be assigned to gates, patrols, escorts, emergency response, Archive protection, or expeditions. There is no lesser posting: a Squire at a gate can do more good than a Knight who never draws a blade.

The order is organized into Squires, Knights, and Captains, each divided into three ranks. Rank measures trust and responsibility, not merely fighting ability.

A Manual for Squires

Issued by order of the New Church of Jonas Falx, Independent City of Temporis.

You are not yet a Knight. You may never be one. There is no shame in that. A wall needs stones and watchmen, not only champions.

Carry this manual. Read it. Mark it. Return to it when the answer is not obvious.

I. What We Are

The Forlorn Hope are the knights of Temporis. Not lords, not a bloodline, and not a reward for birth. We are a duty that anyone can be called to and no one is owed.

We are named for those who came before: the knights who stood on the surface of a dying world while what remained of humanity sheltered beneath it. They held the line when holding it made no sense. We do not worship them. We study what they did right, what they did wrong, and what was lost.

Protect the Weak

Stand between danger and those who would otherwise suffer from it.

Be the Wall

Hold the line when there is still something worth defending.

Preserve Knowledge

Protect records, techniques, names, and knowledge from needless loss.

The scope of the Hope is Temporis, including its people, archives, walls, roads, and future. We are not saviors of the world. Aid beyond the walls may be freely given, but it is not owed.

II. The Hierarchy

There are three stations: Squire, Knight, and Captain. Each contains First, Second, and Third Rank. These are not nine different kinds of soldier. Rank measures trust.

“Rank is not a reward. It is a measure of what we are willing to place in your hands.”
Strength alone does not determine advancement. Judgment, loyalty, conduct, experience, and the trust earned through service matter.

III. On Death, and Why We Do Not Romanticize It

Seraph Return is not a promise. Falling can be survivable and still be painful, costly, and frightening for those left behind. Some true humans have one death, certain.

“Did you fall because you had to? Or because falling felt noble?”

There is no glory in an avoidable fall. Seek people who will still be standing tomorrow, not legends made by dying today.

IV. Duties You Will Be Asked to Carry

Wall and Gate: Maintain the city's defenses and control entry.

Patrol: Watch roads, walls, wards, and areas where danger may emerge.

Escort: Protect people, shipments, and other vulnerable movements.

Archive Detail: Guard restricted records and the people responsible for preserving them.

Emergency Response: Answer urgent threats and crises where the city needs trained hands.

Expedition Support: Accompany expeditions where a decision may determine the survival of a person or the preservation of irreplaceable knowledge.

There is no lesser posting. A Squire at a gate can do more good than a Knight who never draws a blade.

V. The Oath

“I will guard the living. I will preserve the dead. I will defend what is known. I will seek what is not. I will not surrender while there remains something worth saving.”

VI. The Creed

PROTECT. PRESERVE. ENDURE.

Department of Municipal Works

The Department of Municipal Works oversees the wall, the wards, and the ongoing reconstruction of the Cathedral. Its work concerns the maintenance and continued development of the city's public works.

The Wall

The wall is the outer line of defense for Temporis, maintained by Municipal Works and watched over by Forlorn Hope patrols. Gate access and wall repair are coordinated jointly between the two departments.

The Wards

The wards are the districts of Temporis, each with its own upkeep needs. Municipal Works manages infrastructure across the wards, from roadways to shared civic spaces.

Cathedral Reconstruction

Rebuilding of the Cathedral continues, guided by findings from ongoing archaeological surveys of the original structure. Read more on the Cathedral page.

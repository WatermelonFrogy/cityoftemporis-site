Bureau of Public Records and Registry

The Bureau of Public Records and Registry handles residency registration, property filings, and the city's civil ledgers. Matters concerning the registry are handled through the city's established civic offices.

Residency Registration

New residents of Temporis register in person with the Bureau. Registration establishes a resident's standing under the charter and is a prerequisite for property filings and most other civic matters.

Property Filings

The Bureau maintains filings for property held within the city, including transfers, leases, and disputes brought before the registry. Filings extended hours are announced through public notices during periods of high demand.

Civil Ledgers

The city's civil ledgers, a running record of residency, property, and other civic matters, are maintained by the Bureau and cross referenced with the archives kept by the Office of the City Archivist.

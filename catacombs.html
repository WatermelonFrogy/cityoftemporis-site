The Catacombs

The restricted archive beneath Temporis, holding the city's most sensitive records.

What the Catacombs Are

The Catacombs lie beneath Temporis and hold the city's most sensitive records, those the Office of the City Archivist has judged too dangerous, too sensitive, or too easily misused for open circulation. Access has never been unrestricted, though it has historically been available to residents with proper cause and Archivist approval.

Current Restriction

Following reports of rust-touched creatures within the Catacombs, general access has been prohibited. Entry is currently restricted to authorized personnel of the Forlorn Hope, who maintain an Archive Detail specifically tasked with guarding restricted records and the people responsible for preserving them.

Who Guards the Catacombs

The Catacombs are jointly overseen by the Office of the City Archivist and the Forlorn Hope. The Archivist's Office determines what is kept and what may be seen. The Forlorn Hope determines who may safely enter, and stands watch over those who do.

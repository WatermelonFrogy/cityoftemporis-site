City Departments

The offices of Temporis, organized under the city charter.

← Return to the civic portal

Civic life in Temporis is administered through four offices, each chartered with its own duties. The Office of the City Archivist also serves as the seat of government, while the Bureau of Registry, the Department of Municipal Works, and the Forlorn Hope each hold their own charge over the city's records, infrastructure, and defense.

1

Office of the City Archivist

Preserves civic records and written history. The Archivist's Office also serves as the government office of Temporis.

2

Bureau of Public Records and Registry

Handles residency registration, property filings, and the city's civil ledgers.

Department of Municipal Works

The Forlorn Hope

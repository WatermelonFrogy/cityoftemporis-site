Public Notices

Standing bulletins from the city and its departments.

← Return to the civic portal
  • June of Year 6

    Restricted archival access

    Office of the City Archivist

    Following reports of rust-touched creatures, access to restricted archival storage has been temporarily suspended. Entry remains restricted to authorized personnel only.

  • May of Year 6

    Cathedral reconstruction underway

    Department of Municipal Works

    Rebuilding of the Cathedral continues, guided by findings from ongoing archaeological surveys of the original structure. See the Cathedral page for details.

  • April of Year 6

    Forlorn Hope enlistment open

    Department of the Forlorn Hope

    The Hall of the Hope is accepting petitions for the Squire trial. See the recruitment section for eligibility and how to apply.

  • March of Year 6

    Temporis reaffirms independence from the Conclave of Hagia

    Office of the City Archivist

    The city reaffirms that it remains outside the Conclave of Hagia while continuing to welcome trade and diplomacy with all who approach in friendship.

  • August of Year 5

    Wardens requested for wall patrol rotation

    Department of Municipal Works

    The Department of Municipal Works, in coordination with the Forlorn Hope, has called for additional Squires to join the wall patrol rotation.

Immigration and Civic Entry

Temporis is open to newcomers who bring labor, skill, and civic purpose to the city.

← Return to the civic portal

Welcome to Temporis

Temporis is an independent city with a charter and a public system of order, record, and defense. The city welcomes people who can contribute to its life and accept the obligations of maintaining its public peace, infrastructure, and future.

Newcomers are encouraged to register through the appropriate civic offices and to understand the obligations of service, law, and community before taking up residence.

Opportunities for Service and Labor

The city relies on citizens and arrivals who are prepared to contribute across its many work and public duties. The following opportunities are among those commonly available to settlers and applicants:

  • Labourer
  • Clergy
  • Farmer
  • Blacksmith
  • Government Jobs
  • District Mayor
  • Event Staff

These roles represent the kinds of work and civic responsibility that sustain the city. The city values practical work, disciplined service, and a willingness to contribute to the public life of Temporis.

How to Begin

Those seeking to settle in Temporis should begin with the city registry and the public offices responsible for civic record keeping. The Office of the City Archivist, the Bureau of Public Records and Registry, and the Department of Municipal Works are the principal points of contact for orientation and administration.

For those seeking residence, service, or civic office, a petition may be submitted through the city's public petition process.

Living in Temporis

Temporis is a city defined by order, civic memory, and practical labor. The city remains in fellowship with the New Church of Jonas Falx, and many of its institutions are shaped by that relationship, particularly the care of records, archives, and public stability.

Immigration is not simply a matter of arrival. It is a matter of participation. The city asks those who come to it to contribute to its life in honest, useful, public ways.
